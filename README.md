### Featured projects

#### [U.S. Hydropower Generation Forecasting](https://github.com/akwasiagyei7/us-hydro-forecast)
**Python · Time series · Machine learning · Energy data**

- Built a research benchmark using public **U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)** generation records and **U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)** streamflow data for eight hydropower plants.
- Compared statistical baselines and forecasting models with random forest and gradient boosting approaches.
- Evaluated historical predictions over **2020–2024**, including performance during low-streamflow periods.
- Documented the distinction between retrospective analyses using observed current-month flow and forecasts using only historical inputs.

[Explore the code, charts, and results →](https://github.com/akwasiagyei7/us-hydro-forecast)

#### [Amazon Product Ratings and Pricing Analysis](https://github.com/akwasiagyei7/Amazon_e-commerce_analysis)
**Python · pandas · Exploratory data analysis · Visualization**

- Cleaned and explored product-listing data to examine prices, discounts, customer ratings, and review counts.
- Used descriptive statistics and visualizations to compare products, price ranges, and categories.
- Focused on interpreting customer feedback measures accurately rather than treating ratings or review counts as verified sales figures.

[View the analysis notebook →](https://github.com/akwasiagyei7/Amazon_e-commerce_analysis)
